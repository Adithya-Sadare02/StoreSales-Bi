**Power BI Superstore Project – Task Descriptions**
This project uses the Superstore dataset to uncover business insights through interactive Power BI dashboards and reports.

**1. Regional Profitability**
Analyzes sales, profit, and profit margin across regions to identify which region performs best and why.

**2. Discount vs Profit**
Examines the relationship between discounts and profitability to determine if discounts drive sales or hurt margins.

**3. Product Performance**
Highlights top and bottom-performing products based on profit, sales, quantity, and discount to optimize product strategy.

**4. Shipping Efficiency**
Evaluates shipping modes by average delivery time and profit per order to find the most cost-effective option.

**5. Sales Trends**
Tracks sales and profit over time, using forecasting to predict future performance.

**6. Repeat Customers**
Identifies repeat buyers and analyzes their behavior across segments, regions, and categories to understand customer loyalty.

**7. Executive Summary Dashboard**
Provides a high-level overview of key KPIs like total sales, profit, and orders with interactive filters.

**8. Drillthrough Page**
Offers detailed insights on selected items (like customers or products) for granular analysis.
